About

I build the tools I wish research desks already had.

My background spans corporate finance, econometrics, and applied statistics, and I use that foundation to turn raw data into decisions, whether that means regressing news sentiment against market returns or shipping a full-stack app from scratch.

At the University of Michigan I studied Economics with a QMSS focus in data visualization, layered on coursework in corporate finance, international finance, and game theory. Outside the classroom, I managed the Women's Basketball Team, where tracking live performance data under pressure taught me the same discipline I bring to markets: stay composed, trust the process, and let the numbers argue for themselves.

I'm most energized by projects that combine financial reasoning with modern tooling, like Python, Claude Code, and AI-assisted engineering, to move faster than a spreadsheet alone ever could.
